## 1. The San Antonio Market Reality
1. **No State Licensing in Texas:**
   - Texas is one of the few states where roofing contractors do not require a state-issued license.
   - **The Consequence:** Whenever severe hail or wind hits Bexar County (which happens multiple times every spring and summer), out-of-state "storm chasers" flood neighborhoods, knock on doors, make empty promises, collect deposits, and leave.
   - **HomeFirst Advantage:** Highlighting Abraham Cruz as a true San Antonio native with deep community ties (Austin Opera, youth music schools, nonprofit work) establishes instant authenticity and permanence. Homeowners know he isn't packing up a trailer and vanishing next month.

2. **Texas House Bill 2102 (Deductible Law):**
   - Texas law strictly prohibits roofers from waiving, rebating, or paying a homeowner's insurance deductible.
   - Shady contractors still promise "free roofs" by illicitly waiving deductibles, which can invalidate insurance claims or commit fraud.
   - **HomeFirst Advantage:** Educating homeowners on HB 2102 positions HomeFirst as an ethical, legal ally who navigates insurance honestly and gets legitimate storm claims approved through thorough photo/drone documentation.

3. **South Texas Climate Demands:**
   - 100°F+ extreme summer heat destroys cheap shingles; golf ball hail cracks standard 3-tab roofs.
   - **HomeFirst Advantage:** Promoting **Class 4 Impact-Resistant Shingles** (SBS rubberized) and **Radiant Barrier / Cool Roof Ventilation** offers tangible ROI through lower CPS Energy bills and 15%–28% annual homeowners insurance discounts.

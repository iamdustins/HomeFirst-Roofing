# Wix Setup & Publishing Guide for HomeFirst Roofing

This guide gives you two easy ways to launch your HomeFirst Roofing website on Wix:

---

## Method 1: The 60-Second Direct Wix Embed (Fastest & 100% Visual Match)

If you love the **glossy smoky overlays, interactive cost calculator, hail damage self-checker, and Abraham Cruz spotlight** we just built, you can embed the entire webpage directly inside Wix with zero coding.

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. **Log in to [Wix.com](https://www.wix.com)** and open your site inside the **Wix Editor** or **Wix Studio**.
2. Click the **`+` (Add Elements)** button on the left sidebar.
3. Select **Embed Code** > **Popular Embeds** > **Embed HTML** (or **Custom Element** / **HTML iFrame**).
4. Click **Enter Code**.
5. Open `h:\Projects\HOME_SAFE_ROOFING\index.html` on your computer, press `Ctrl + A` (select all), `Ctrl + C` (copy), and paste it directly into the Wix code box.
6. Click **Apply**.
7. In the Wix layout settings for that block, set width to **Full Width (100%)** and adjust the height to fit.
8. Click **Publish** at the top right of Wix. Your site is live!

Granule Loss in Gutters

Finding what looks like black coffee grounds or dark sand piling up around your downspouts after rain.

Dents on AC Units or Vents

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything San Antonio homeowners need to know about roof integrity, insurance laws, and materials.

Does Texas require roofing licenses, and how does HomeFirst protect me?

In Texas, the state unfortunately does not license roofers. That is why verifying local business roots, general liability insurance, and worker’s compensation is critical. HomeFirst Roofing is fully insured, locally owned by San Antonian Abraham Cruz, and backed by manufacturer-certified master installers with 30+ combined years of experience.

Can a roofer "waive" or pay my insurance deductible in Texas?

No. Under Texas House Bill 2102, it is a Class B misdemeanor for a contractor to pay, waive, rebate, or absorb a homeowner's insurance deductible. Any roofer who promises to "eat your deductible" is committing insurance fraud, which can jeopardize your entire claim. HomeFirst operates with 100% legal transparency and helps you maximize fair claim coverage without illegal shortcuts.

What are Class 4 impact shingles and do they lower my insurance?

Class 4 shingles pass the UL 2218 steel ball drop test, simulating 2-inch hail stones traveling at 90 mph without cracking. Most major Texas insurance carriers (State Farm, USAA, Allstate, Farmers, etc.) offer substantial premium discounts—often 15% to 30% per year—when you install a qualifying Class 4 roof.

How long does a full roof replacement take?

Most residential roofs in San Antonio are completed in just a single day! Our crew begins at sunrise, tears off the old shingles, inspects the wood decking, installs synthetic underlayment and new shingles, and finishes with multiple magnetic sweep sweeps of your driveway and yard so no nails are left behind.

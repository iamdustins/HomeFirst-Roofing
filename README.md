## 🌟 Overview & Brand Highlights

This repository contains the high-end landing page, assets, Wix integration guides, and competitor strategy for **HomeFirst Roofing**.

* **Brand Colors**: 
  - Primary Navy: `#0B2546`
  - Smoky Dark Charcoal: `#061629`
  - Terracotta Safety Orange: `#C7532B`
  - Pure Crisp White: `#FFFFFF`
* **Visual Aesthetic**: Glossy frosted glassmorphism cards (`backdrop-blur-xl`), crisp glowing borders, and smoky dark photographic overlays providing dimensional visual depth.
* **Community Story**: Highlights founder Abraham Cruz's deep San Antonio roots, his dedication to local youth music schools, support for Austin Opera initiatives, and his nonprofit assisting children in need.
* **Competitor Strategy**: Purpose-built to counter fly-by-night, out-of-town "storm chasers" following Texas hail events by emphasizing true local ownership, Texas HB 2102 insurance compliance, and lifetime craftsmanship warranties.

---

## 🛠️ Key Features Included

1. **Interactive San Antonio Roof Cost Estimator**:
   - Live square footage slider (1,200 – 4,500+ sq ft).
   - Toggles between Architectural Shingles, UL 2218 Class 4 Hail Shingles, and Standing Seam Metal.
   - Calculates estimated price ranges and Texas homeowner insurance premium discounts.
2. **5-Point Hail Damage Self-Checker**:
   - Interactive ground-level checklist for homeowners to spot granule loss, AC unit dings, and lifted shingles without climbing on the roof.
3. **Storm Chasers vs. HomeFirst Comparison Defense Table**:
   - Transparently contrasts transient contractors against HomeFirst's licensed, insured, local crew.
4. **21-Point Drone Inspection Booking Forms**:
   - Hero fast-track form, main scheduler, and instant confirmation modal.
5. **Brand Assets**:
   - High-resolution SVG vector logo (`assets/logo.svg`).
   - Original brand image asset (`assets/logo.png`).
